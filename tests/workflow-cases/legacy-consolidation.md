# Legacy guidance consolidation scenario

Outcome-based extension of the existing first-cut, same-Clip feedback, and
with/without-skills cases. This scenario checks the retained editorial judgment
without requiring legacy wording, fixed presets, or host-specific tool
choreography. Host behavior has not yet been tested.

## Setup

Use the same rights-scoped fixture in two bounded tasks:

1. A multi-segment narrative or film whose throughline depends on material from
   more than one part of the Episode.
2. A focused thematic Clip whose useful context is local to one neighborhood.

Provide a current Project working brief, then give a newer request that changes
one editorial priority. Include at least one speaker whose attribution is not
verified, one optional visual/audio treatment, and one same-Clip material edit
after a ready exact Export. Do not prescribe timestamps, sentence-start words,
presets, camera durations, or publication destinations.

## Expected outcomes

- The narrative task reads enough of the whole Episode to build a coherent arc;
  the themed task uses focused context without loading unrelated material.
- The latest request governs when it conflicts with the older working brief.
  The assistant does not invent a default Project, person, destination, or
  treatment.
- Each cut preserves complete ideas and understandable referents. Editorial
  quality is judged from the actual result, not a banned-word list.
- No speaker title is added until its attribution is verified. Optional
  captions, titles, camera, framing, audio, music, intro, or outro choices are
  included only when they serve the request or working brief.
- Frame/media inspection checks framing, caption/title collisions, camera
  behavior, and both sides of changed seams. Total duration includes any
  inserted intro, outro, or other treatment.
- Material feedback revises the same Clip and retains the prior exact Export.
  The reviewer inspects the affected region before the edit and compares the
  new exact Export afterward, stating any audio, motion, or playback limitation.
- Draft-only remains draft-only; ordinary finished-result intent may Render once
  its existing prerequisites pass. Publication still uses the server-owned
  exact-Export, named-destination confirmation boundary.

## Failure conditions

- A universal read-only approval phase, forced suggestion persistence, or an
  extra approval ritual before ordinary private rendering.
- Refusing to revise the existing Clip, replacing it without alternative intent,
  or losing the prior Export.
- Automatic captions, music, titles, outro, aspect ratio, or social posts that
  were not justified by the current request or working brief.
- Personal-name or destination assumptions, fixed gallery/Zoom timing, banned
  sentence starters, or hardcoded preset/API recipes used as editorial proof.
- Transcript-only boundary authority, invented timestamps, raw media URLs, or a
  claim of watched/listened quality when the media was unavailable.

## Required evidence

- Reading scope and a concise account of why it fit each task.
- Current request, working-brief conflict, and observed chosen priority.
- Speaker-attribution state and the resulting title decision.
- Before/after exact Export identities, same Clip revisions, retained prior
  Export, and watched/listened notes around the changed material.
- Treatment/collision/duration observations and explicit unverified media limits.
- No duplicate write, unrequested publication, or legacy-only instruction set.
